AQUAGUARD AI — WATER CONSUMPTION ANOMALY INSPECTION PROCEDURES

Purpose:
This document provides a structured procedure for investigating abnormal water-consumption patterns identified by AquaGuard AI.

1. Confirm the Detected Anomaly

Review the available monitoring information:

- Actual consumption
- Expected consumption
- Percentage deviation
- Anomaly status
- Consecutive anomaly count
- Risk classification
- Historical consumption

The purpose is to understand whether the abnormal pattern is isolated or persistent.

2. Check for Legitimate Water Usage

Before assuming that a technical problem exists, identify legitimate reasons for increased consumption.

Possible explanations include:
- Increased number of users
- Cleaning activities
- Irrigation
- Visitors
- Appliance operation
- Tank filling
- Maintenance
- Scheduled water usage
- Changes in facility operation

A legitimate increase should not automatically be classified as leakage.

3. Check the Water Meter

Where safe and appropriate:

1. Stop known water-consuming activities.
2. Observe the water meter.
3. Check whether the meter continues to register water flow.
4. Record the observation.

Continued meter movement may indicate unintended consumption, but further investigation is required.

4. Inspect Visible Fixtures

Inspect accessible fixtures for signs of unintended water flow.

Check:
- Taps
- Toilets
- Shower fixtures
- Pipes
- Pipe joints
- Valves
- Hoses
- Storage tanks
- Water connections

Look for:
- Dripping
- Continuous flow
- Wet areas
- Water pooling
- Overflow
- Damp surfaces
- Unusual sounds

5. Check High-Contributing Sources

If AquaGuard identifies a particular consumption source as dominant, investigate that source first.

For example:

Bathroom:
Check toilets, taps, showers, and visible pipes.

Kitchen:
Check taps, sinks, pipes, and connected appliances.

Irrigation:
Check irrigation schedules, pipes, valves, and sprinkler systems.

Storage:
Check tanks, inlet valves, outlet valves, and overflow conditions.

6. Compare with Historical Patterns

Compare the current consumption with:
- Previous hours
- Previous days
- Previous weeks
- Similar operating periods

A repeated pattern provides stronger evidence for investigation than a single isolated spike.

7. Investigate Persistent Anomalies

If abnormal consumption continues for several consecutive observations, increase the priority of investigation.

AquaGuard should consider:
- Severity of deviation
- Duration
- Consecutive anomaly count
- Major contributing source
- Historical pattern

8. Critical Anomaly Response

For a critical anomaly:

1. Review the consumption measurements.
2. Check whether legitimate high usage occurred.
3. Check the meter where safe.
4. Inspect visible fixtures.
5. Investigate the major contributing source.
6. Monitor whether the abnormal flow continues.
7. Contact qualified maintenance personnel when necessary.

9. Professional Inspection

If the source cannot be safely identified or the abnormal condition persists, users should consider inspection by qualified maintenance personnel or the relevant water-service provider.

AquaGuard should not instruct users to dismantle:
- Pressurized plumbing
- Electrical equipment
- Water meters
- Specialized infrastructure

unless they are appropriately qualified.

10. Record Inspection Results

Where possible, record:
- Inspection time
- Suspected source
- Observed condition
- Corrective action
- Post-correction consumption

This information can support future monitoring and evaluation.

11. Responsible AI Guidance

AquaGuard must distinguish between:

Observed:
"What the monitoring system measured."

Inferred:
"What the consumption pattern may indicate."

Confirmed:
"What physical inspection establishes."

The AI must not present an inferred leakage source as a confirmed physical fault.
