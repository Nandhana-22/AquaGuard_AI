AQUAGUARD AI — WATER CONSERVATION PRACTICES

Purpose:
This document contains practical water-conservation practices that AquaGuard AI can recommend based on detected consumption patterns.

1. Monitor Water Consumption

Regular monitoring helps identify:
- Sudden increases
- Long-term increases
- Repeated abnormal consumption
- Unusual consumption during low-demand periods

Comparing current consumption with historical and expected consumption can help users identify potential problems early.

2. Fix Leaking Fixtures

Leaking taps, toilets, pipes, valves, and other fixtures can cause unnecessary water loss.

Users should regularly inspect visible plumbing fixtures and arrange repairs when leakage is suspected.

3. Use Water-Efficient Fixtures

Water-efficient fixtures can reduce unnecessary water consumption.

Examples include:
- Efficient taps
- Low-flow showerheads
- Water-efficient toilets
- Efficient irrigation equipment

The suitability of a fixture depends on the location and intended use.

4. Reduce Unnecessary Running Water

Users should avoid leaving taps running when water is not actively required.

Examples:
- Turn off taps while brushing teeth.
- Avoid unnecessary running water while cleaning.
- Use only the amount of water required for an activity.
- Repair dripping fixtures.

5. Improve Bathing Efficiency

Water consumption during bathing can be reduced by:
- Reducing shower duration
- Avoiding unnecessary water flow
- Using efficient shower fixtures
- Turning off water when it is not required

6. Use Appliances Efficiently

Water-consuming appliances should be operated efficiently.

Examples:
- Run washing machines with appropriate loads.
- Avoid unnecessary repeated cycles.
- Use efficient settings when available.
- Maintain appliances to prevent abnormal water consumption.

7. Improve Irrigation Efficiency

Outdoor water use can be reduced by:
- Watering plants according to their actual needs
- Avoiding unnecessary irrigation
- Using efficient irrigation methods
- Monitoring irrigation schedules
- Checking irrigation pipes and connections for leakage

8. Rainwater Harvesting

Rainwater harvesting can help supplement available water resources where appropriate.

A suitable rainwater harvesting system may:
- Collect rainwater
- Store water for later use
- Reduce dependence on supplied water
- Support non-potable uses where safe and permitted

Local regulations, water quality, system design, and hygiene requirements should be considered.

9. Safe Water Reuse

Where permitted and safe, suitable water may be reused for non-potable purposes.

Examples can include certain cleaning or gardening applications.

Water reuse should only be recommended when:
- The water quality is suitable.
- The intended use is safe.
- Applicable regulations are followed.

10. Reduce Water Waste

Users should identify activities where water is consumed unnecessarily.

AquaGuard can help prioritize conservation actions based on observed consumption patterns.

11. AquaGuard Recommendation Strategy

Normal:
Recommend general water-awareness and conservation practices.

Warning:
Encourage monitoring and review of recent water-consuming activities.

High:
Identify major contributing sources and recommend targeted conservation measures.

Critical:
Prioritize investigation of possible unintended water loss before recommending general conservation measures.

12. Responsible AI Guidance

AquaGuard should not promise a specific amount of water savings unless that estimate is supported by reliable data.

Avoid statements such as:
"You will definitely save 500 litres."

Prefer:
"This action may help reduce unnecessary water consumption."

Recommendations should be practical, evidence-based, and appropriate to the detected situation.
